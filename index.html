ABOUT DAIMA MABATI

Roofing Solutions Made for Modern Construction

Daima Mabati Eldoret supplies a wide range of modern roofing materials designed to combine durability, appearance and practical performance.

From clean contemporary box profiles to classic corrugated sheets, Roman Tile, Versatile and premium stone coated roofing, customers can choose from multiple profiles, colours and specifications to match their project.

Based in Eldoret near Rupa's Mall, Daima Mabati serves homeowners, contractors, developers and businesses in Eldoret and beyond, with delivery available across Kenya.

Whether you are roofing a new home, upgrading an existing property or sourcing materials for a larger construction project, our team is ready to help you choose the right roofing solution.

OUR PRODUCTS

Modern Roofing Profiles for Every Project

From practical everyday roofing to premium architectural finishes, choose a profile that matches your property's design, budget and performance requirements.

01
BOX PROFILE

Box Profile Mabati

A sleek trapezoidal profile designed for modern homes, commercial buildings and industrial structures. Its clean geometric appearance creates a contemporary roofline while providing effective water shedding and practical durability. Available in different colours and finishes, including glossy and premium matte options where available.

Enquire About Box Profile
02
CORRUGATED PROFILE

Corrugated Mabati

The classic wavy roofing profile remains a practical choice for residential buildings, agricultural structures, fencing and cost conscious projects. Known for its familiar appearance, easy installation and dependable performance.

Enquire About Corrugated Mabati
03
ROMAN TILE

Roman Tile

Bring the elegant appearance of traditional tiled roofing to your project with a lightweight steel roofing profile. Roman Tile is particularly suited to homes and properties where the roof is an important part of the overall architectural appearance.

Enquire About Roman Tile
04
VERSATILE

Versatile Mabati

A stylish stepped profile that delivers the visual character of tiled roofing while retaining the practical advantages of modern steel roofing. An excellent option for contemporary residential and suburban properties.

Enquire About Versatile
05
DECRA / STONE COATED

Stone Coated Roofing

A premium roofing solution featuring interlocking steel panels with a stone coated finish, designed for customers looking for a distinctive premium appearance combined with durability, weather resistance and low maintenance. The stone coated surface can provide a quieter, more refined roofing experience compared with conventional metal roofing, depending on the roof structure and installation.

Enquire About Stone Coated Roofing
